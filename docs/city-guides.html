City guides

Where to eat vegetarian, city by city

Curated, not crowd-sourced: neighbourhood picks and price bands. Restaurants can apply to be listed.

🗽

New York

East Village and Williamsburg lead; Indian on Lexington, Ethiopian in Harlem. $$–$$$.

🎡

London

Shoreditch, Camden, Brixton. Gujarati canteens in Wembley are the value play. ££.

🌴

Los Angeles

Silver Lake, Venice, Culver City; Thai Town for the easiest vegan menus.

🍁

Toronto

Kensington Market, Queen West; Gerrard India Bazaar for South Asian veg.

🐻

Berlin

Neukölln and Kreuzberg; Europe's densest vegan restaurant scene per capita.

🏛️

Montréal

Plateau and Mile End; Little Italy for vegetarian-friendly trattorias. $$.

🕌

Mumbai

Vegetarian by default — Matunga for South Indian, Ghatkopar for Gujarati street food. $.

🗼

Tokyo

Shojin (temple) cuisine in Asakusa; check dashi (fish stock) in ramen and miso.

Chains that are reliably safe

Chipotle sofritas, Taco Bell fresco-style, Panera Mediterranean veggie, most pizza doughs, and any Indian, Thai, Ethiopian or Middle-Eastern restaurant where a third of the menu is vegetarian by default. Full list in the eating-out guide.
