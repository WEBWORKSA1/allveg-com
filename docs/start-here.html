FAQ

Will I get enough protein?

Yes, if you plan it. Most people need 1.2–1.6 g per kg of body weight when active; tofu, tempeh, lentils, beans and soy milk make that routine. Use the calculator.

Do I need supplements?

Vegans: B12, always. Everyone: vitamin D in winter; algae omega-3 is sensible. Details in the four-nutrient guide.

Is it more expensive?

Beans, lentils, oats and rice are the cheapest calories in the store. Our $40-a-week plan proves it.

What about eating out and family dinners?

See the eating-out cheat sheet and the family guide.
