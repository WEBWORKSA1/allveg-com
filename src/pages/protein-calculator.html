How the calculator works

Protein requirements scale with body weight and training load. Sedentary adults do well at 0.8–1.0 g/kg; anyone training regularly benefits from 1.2–1.6 g/kg; strength athletes and people in a calorie deficit protect muscle best at 1.6–2.2 g/kg. Plant proteins are slightly less digestible than animal proteins, so we sit at the upper-middle of each range.

Spread intake across 3–4 meals with 25–40 g each and prioritise leucine-rich sources — soy, lentils, peas, seitan — to reliably cross the ~2.5 g leucine threshold that triggers muscle protein synthesis.
