About

All things vegetarian, done properly

AllVeg exists because plant-based eating has a content problem: endless recipes, almost no tools, and nutrition advice that ranges from vague to wrong.

What we do differently

  • Tools first. A substitution finder, protein calculator, meal planner and comparison hub — free, no login.
  • Evidence. Nutrition guides cite position papers and trials; a registered dietitian reviews them.
  • Costed recipes. Every recipe shows time, protein and calories, and most come in under $3 a serving.
  • Community that pays back. Contests with real prizes, paid briefs for contributors, and revenue share for ambassadors.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense and direct), clearly labelled sponsored content, affiliate commissions on comparison pages, voluntary memberships and donations, and business listings. None of it changes a ranking or a verdict. Details in the disclosure.

Editorial standards

Recipes are tested at least twice before publishing. Nutrition is estimated from standard databases and labelled as such. Corrections are made promptly and noted on the page.
