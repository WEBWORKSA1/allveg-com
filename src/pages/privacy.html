Privacy policy

This policy explains what AllVeg.com ("we") collects and why. We keep it short because we collect little.

What we collect

  • Forms. When you submit a form (newsletter, challenge, contest, application, enquiry) we receive the details you enter. Submissions are delivered to our single operations inbox and used only to respond to you and provide what you asked for.
  • Local storage. Saved recipes, meal plans and preferences are stored in your browser only; they are never sent to us.
  • Analytics. If enabled, Google Analytics collects anonymised usage data (pages viewed, device type, approximate location).
  • Advertising. Google AdSense and its partners use cookies to serve ads, including personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and learn more at How Google uses data.
  • Embedded content. YouTube videos load only when you click them and use youtube-nocookie.com. Payment links open the provider's site; we never see payment details.

Cookies

We use a cookie/local-storage flag to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice or popup, analytics cookies if enabled, and advertising cookies set by Google and its certified vendors. EU/UK visitors are shown consent controls as required.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you submitted by using the contact form. Unsubscribe links are in every email. We do not sell personal data.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EU) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new date. Last updated: September 2026.
